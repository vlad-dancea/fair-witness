# Resident wellbeing survey

Answered online by residents of Favoriten, Ottakring and Floridsdorf in July 2026. The export
holds a random response id, the district, an age band and the answers, without names or contact
details.

1. How many hours did you sleep last night?
2. How much did the heat bother you today, from 1 (not at all) to 5 (very much)?
3. Did you spend time in a park, a cooled room or by the water today?
